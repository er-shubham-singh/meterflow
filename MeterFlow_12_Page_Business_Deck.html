SLIDE 06 / 12

Arbitrarily Complex Consumption Math at Scale

Define any billing metric without writing custom map-reduce jobs or database triggers.

FORMULA 01 SUM
Cumulative Total
LLM Prompt/Completion Tokens, Storage GB-Hours, Transaction Volume in ₹, or bytes transferred over CDN.
FORMULA 02 COUNT
Discrete Occurrence
API Calls executed, Webhooks delivered, SMS notifications sent, or database queries evaluated.
FORMULA 03 MAX
Peak Utilization
Peak concurrent seats, highest RAM allocation, peak bandwidth spike, or active channel capacity in a cycle.
FORMULA 04 UNIQUE
Distinct Cardinality
Distinct monthly active user IDs (MAU), unique visited tenant domains, unique customer IP addresses.
FORMULA 05 WEIGHTED_DURATION
Time × Multiplier
GPU cluster compute seconds multiplied by hardware class coefficient (e.g. Nvidia A100 vs H100 GPU tiers).
FORMULA 06 RATE_WINDOW
Throughput Bursts
Price dynamic traffic bursts over rolling 60-second sliding windows to penalize abusive traffic spikes.
If your system can emit an event, MeterFlow can meter, aggregate, and price it instantly.
